# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report a suspected vulnerability privately, through GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. That
creates a private advisory that only the maintainer can see.

Please do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion about it, and do not include real
personal data, credentials or tokens in the report. Describe what you found, how to reproduce it,
and what you think the impact is.

You will get an acknowledgement, and the advisory will be kept up to date as the report is
investigated and fixed. A fix to deployed code is prepared privately and published only once it
is deployed.

## Scope

This repository holds the source of Fin's rebuild. Testing must stay within your own accounts and
data: no denial of service, no social engineering, no access to other people's data, and no
automated scanning of Fin's hosts.

## No bounty

There is no bug bounty programme, and no reward is promised for a report. Good-faith reports are
welcome and appreciated.
